Privacy Policy

What this site touches, what it doesn't, and how you can control it. Last updated: November 2, 2026.

The short version

YSGame is a static website. There is no account system, no back-end database, and no form asking for personal information. The progress you make in a game is stored in your own browser and is never uploaded — there is no server here to upload it to.

Ads on the site are served by a third party (Google AdSense). That part involves cookies and is described separately below.

1. What we don't collect

This site does not ask for, and does not collect:

  • Your name, gender, age or address
  • Your phone number or email address (unless you choose to write to us)
  • Accounts or passwords — there is no sign-up
  • Payment details — nothing here costs money
  • Access to your contacts, photos or location

2. Local storage in your browser

So that you can close the tab and come back to the same puzzle, the site uses your browser's localStorage. Think of it as a small drawer inside your browser. The data stays on your device.

What is stored

  • ysgame-sudoku-state — the current board, level, elapsed time and hints used, so you can resume
  • sudoku-stats — how many puzzles you have finished at each level, and your best times
  • ysgame-2048-state — the current 2048 board and score, so you can resume
  • ysgame-2048-best — your best 2048 score
  • ysgame-mines-state — the current Minesweeper field, opened squares, flags and time, so you can resume
  • ysgame-mines-best — your best Minesweeper time on each board size
  • ys_sound — whether sound is on or off
  • ys_lang — whether you prefer Chinese or English

Removing it

Clear this site's data in your browser settings and all of the above is deleted. Your saved games and records go with it, permanently. We cannot access this data and cannot recover it for you.

3. Advertising and cookies

This site displays ads through Google AdSense, which is the only income covering the domain and hosting.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies.
  • Opting out does not remove ads. It only makes them unrelated to your browsing history.
  • For how Google uses data on partner sites, see Google's explanation.
Ad slots are filled automatically by Google. This site does not read, store or sell anything held in those cookies, and cannot identify which visitor saw which ad.

4. Server logs

The site does not run its own analytics. The hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard access logs — request time, page address, browser type and coarse region — to keep the service running and to prevent abuse. Those logs are handled under the provider's own privacy terms.

5. Children's privacy

The games here are suitable for all ages, including children. Because the site has no accounts and does not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers, no profile of a child is ever created.

If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal details (for example, a full name and address in an email), write to us and we will delete the message and any related record once verified.

6. Links to other sites

Some pages link to third-party sites, such as Weibo or X when you use the share button, or Google's ad settings. Those sites have their own privacy policies. This site does not control their content or how they handle data.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Since we do not collect or store personal information, there is no user database to leak. The one thing worth remembering: saved game progress lives on your own device, so anyone using the same browser profile can see it.

8. Changes to this policy

If the site gains a feature that touches data — a new analytics tool, a new ad format — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. If privacy matters to you, it's worth a glance now and then.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete data, can be sent to hello@ysgame.xyz.

We aim to reply within seven working days.
