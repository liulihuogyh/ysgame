YS is my daughter's name

This site isn't named after a brand or an idea. It's named after a small person — seventeen months old, and not yet able to read this page.

Where the name comes from

Her name is Yunshu (云舒). We spent a long time on it and landed on two characters from an old line: 云卷云舒 — clouds gather, clouds drift apart.

The wish inside it is simple. That when she grows up, on good days and bad ones alike, she can meet them the way clouds meet weather: unhurried, and still able to unfold.

YS is the first letters of Yunshu in pinyin. It's also why this site sits at ysgame.xyz.

In one line: a small arcade, built by one father in his spare time, named after his daughter. A present that gets to grow.

Why I made it

I like small games. But open an app store and most of what you find works the same way: an energy bar, a daily check-in, watch an ad to continue, log in seven days running for a reward. Ten minutes of playing, eight interruptions.

So I wanted the opposite version. No account, no sign-in, no energy, no leaderboard, no "watch this ad to keep going." Open a game and play. Close the tab whenever. Your progress sits in your own browser. Nobody asks you to come back.

Every game here has to pass the same test: the rules fit in one sentence, there's room to let your mind wander while you play, and you should feel less tired afterwards, not more. That's what "unwind" is supposed to mean.

What's here, and what isn't

Here

  • Sudoku, 2048, Minesweeper, Jigsaw — games worth playing more than once
  • Works on phone, tablet and desktop, portrait or landscape
  • Soft colours and sounds, with a mute button that remembers you
  • Progress saved in your browser, so you can pick up where you stopped
  • Free. No paywall, no in-app purchases

Not here

  • No accounts. No phone number, no email address
  • No leaderboards or daily quests. Nobody is racing you
  • No pop-ups, no "share to unlock", no "invite three friends"
  • No silent upload of what you do here

To be straight with you: there are a few ads. They're the only income, and they cover the domain and the hosting. No pop-ups, nothing covering the board.

How it grew

  • Autumn 2026 A name Bought ysgame.xyz and decided to make something small that could last.
  • Autumn 2026 First game: Sudoku Built the board, the puzzle generator, the timer, hints and saving from scratch. Rewrote it more than once.
  • Winter 2026 The arcade opens Turned the single game page into a front door. Every new game adds one card.
  • Next 2048, Minesweeper, Jigsaw One at a time, no deadline. Finished ones go live.

About me

One person. A day job, and this in the evenings. No team, no company, and no plan to turn it into a product — it's closer to a craft project that happens to live on the internet.

If you find a bug, or have a game you think belongs here, write to hello@ysgame.xyz. I read everything.
