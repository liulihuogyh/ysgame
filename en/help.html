How to play

Every game here should be explainable in a sentence. This page is the longer version, for when something isn't obvious.

Before you start

  • No sign-up. Open the front page, pick a game, you're in.
  • Progress saves itself. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, the board is where you left it. The data lives in your own browser.
  • Phone, tablet or desktop. Portrait and landscape both work. On a computer you can also use the keyboard.
  • Sound can be turned off. The speaker icon in the top bar. Your choice is remembered.

Sudoku: the rules

The board is a 9×9 grid, divided by heavier lines into nine 3×3 boxes. The goal: fill every empty cell so that each row, each column and each 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

The numbers already on the board are fixed. You can't change them, and you don't need to. Every valid puzzle has a single solution — you never have to guess, only reason.

What the reasoning actually looks like

Take a row that already shows 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9. Two cells are left, so they must be 2 and 6. Now look at the columns those cells sit in: one of the columns already has a 2, so that cell has to be 6, and the other is 2. That's the whole game — narrowing until only one answer is left.

One tip: don't start filling. First scan the rows, columns and boxes that already have the most numbers — they usually hand you a few cells for free.

Controls

Mouse or finger

  • Tap an empty cell, then tap a number from 1 to 9
  • Undo steps back one move, Erase clears the selected cell
  • Hint fills one cell for you — three per game
  • Check marks wrong cells in red. It tells you where, never what

Keyboard

  • 1 to 9 to enter a number
  • Backspace or Delete to clear the cell
  • Ctrl + Z to undo

2048: the rules

A 4×4 board, starting with two tiles. Slide up, down, left or right and every tile travels as far as it can in that direction. Two tiles with the same number merge into one, doubling the value. Each slide adds a new 2 (sometimes a 4) to an empty square.

The goal is 2048. You can keep going afterwards — 4096, 8192, whatever you like. Nothing here is timed. The game ends only when the board is full and no direction changes anything.

Controls

Phone or tablet: swipe anywhere on the board. Short swipes count.

Keyboard:

  • ↑ ↓ ← →, or W A S D
  • Ctrl + Z to undo, up to twenty moves back
A small tip: park your largest tile in one corner and only slide along the two directions that touch it. Slide in all four directions and the big numbers drift into the middle.

Your score is the sum of every tile created by merging. The best score is kept separately and survives a new game. Close the tab whenever you like — the board is saved and picks up where you left it.

Questions

If I close the tab, do I lose my progress?

No. Every move is saved to your browser's local storage and restored when you come back, timer included.

Two things will lose it: clearing this site's data in your browser, or switching device or browser. The save belongs to one browser, and doesn't sync.

Why are my records gone on another device?

Because there's no account system. Progress is stored locally, not in the cloud. That's the trade for not asking you for an email address.

I made a mistake but can't find it

Press Check. Wrong cells turn red. It tells you which ones, not what the answer should be — that part is still yours.

If you're genuinely stuck, Hint fills one cell. You get three per game.

Does it work offline?

The first visit needs a connection. After that, as long as your browser cache is intact, the page will usually still open offline — the game itself doesn't need the network at all.

Are there ads? Do they cover the board?

A few, above and below the content. They're the only income and they pay for the domain and hosting. No pop-ups, nothing over the board, nothing interrupting a game.

Ads are filled by a third party, so I don't choose what appears. See the privacy policy for how the cookies work.

The board doesn't fit on my phone

Try reloading the page first. If it still looks wrong, tell me your phone model and browser and I'll fix it properly.

What games are coming next?

The order is 2048, then Minesweeper, then Jigsaw, then a match-three and Snake. One at a time, and each one goes live when it's finished. If you want something sooner, say so — loud requests move up the list.

Can I share this site?

Please do. Just send ysgame.xyz. No invite codes, no "share to unlock" — it's all open.

Something broken, or an idea?

Write to hello@ysgame.xyz. Bugs, display problems, games you think belong here — all welcome.

If it's a display problem, these four things save a lot of back-and-forth:

  • Phone or computer, and the model
  • Which browser (Chrome, Safari, the in-app browser of a social app…)
  • Which game, and what you tapped just before it happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

I read everything. Usually a reply within one to three days.
